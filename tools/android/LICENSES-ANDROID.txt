SMRPG Recomp for Android: what goes inside the APK, and its licenses
==============================================================================

None of this belongs to the game: the code and data of Super Mario RPG come from your own copy. A fan project, not
affiliated with Nintendo or Square Enix; Super Mario RPG is a trademark of Nintendo.

The port
  libmain.so: based on LAS Recomp (MIT license, LAS-Recomp-LICENSE.txt). Inside there are also Dear ImGui (MIT,
  DearImGui-LICENSE.txt), SMAA (MIT, SMAA-LICENSE.txt) and the shaders of Qualcomm's Snapdragon Game Super Resolution
  (3-clause BSD, Snapdragon-GSR-LICENSE.txt; https://github.com/SnapdragonStudios/snapdragon-gsr), AMD's Vulkan Memory
  Allocator (MIT, VMA-LICENSE.txt), the audio ported from Ryujinx's (MIT, Ryujinx-LICENSE.txt) and AMD FSR 1 ported to
  GLSL (MIT: ffx_fsr1.h in AMD-FSR-SDK-LICENSE.md).

Libraries (built from their sources with tools/android/deps.sh)
  SDL3 3.4.20 (zlib license) ......................... SDL3-LICENSE.txt
  FFmpeg 9.0.2: avcodec, avformat, avutil, swscale .. LGPL 2.1 or later: FFmpeg-LGPLv2.1.txt. Built without anything GPL,
                                                      with only the decoders the movies use. Source code:
                                                      https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-9.0.2.tar.xz (also attached to
                                                      the release); its configuration is in the port's
                                                      tools/android/deps.sh.
  Opus 1.6.1 (3-clause BSD) .......................... Opus-COPYING.txt
  shaderc 2026.4, with glslang 16.6.0 and SPIRV-Tools
  (Apache 2.0 / BSD) ................................. shaderc-LICENSE.txt, glslang-LICENSE.txt, SPIRV-Tools-LICENSE.txt
  libc++ from the Android NDK (Apache 2.0 with the
  LLVM exceptions) ................................... LLVM-libcxx-NOTICE.txt

Lossless Scaling frame generation (liblsfg.so, built from its sources with tools/lsfg/build.sh)
  lsfg-vk (GameNative's Android branch, MIT) ......... lsfg-vk-LICENSE.txt
  (https://github.com/GameNative/lsfg-vk-android), with DXVK's DXBC translator (zlib, DXVK-dxbc-LICENSE.txt), pe-parse
  (MIT, pe-parse-LICENSE.txt) and volk (MIT, volk-LICENSE.txt).
  LSFG's shaders are not here: they come from the Lossless.dll of your copy of Lossless Scaling (a paid program on Steam),
  which you copy to the game's data folder.

Fonts
  Baloo 2 and BIZ UDPGothic (SIL Open Font License) .. Baloo2-OFL.txt, BIZUDPGothic-OFL.txt
  DejaVu Sans (Bitstream Vera / DejaVu license) ...... DejaVu-LICENSE.txt
